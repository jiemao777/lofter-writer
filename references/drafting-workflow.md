# Drafting Workflow

Use this reference for planning, drafting, continuation, revision, or a full LOFTER publish pack.

## Intake

Collect the smallest useful set of facts:

- IP or original world
- Characters, CP, relationship direction, or character focus
- Desired feeling in plain language
- Hard boundaries, disliked tropes, rating limit, and canon sensitivity
- Target length and standalone, chapter, or series intent
- Posting goal and known fandom tag customs
- Whether AI materially assists the publishable work

If the user has no idea, offer three directions with different emotional payoffs. Do not require literary terminology before starting.

## Route

Choose a format from reading intent:

- Fast and funny: forum thread, chat log, comedy bit, group reaction
- Sweet and easy: daily scene, short vignette, domestic moment, soft confession
- Plot and payoff: canon drama, serial chapter, identity reveal, mission structure
- Public reaction: viewing/reaction, public reveal, forum live thread
- Long-term collection: serial outline with stable motifs, chapter hooks, and continuity files

Read `writing-mode-playbooks.md` before executing a specialized format.

## Plan

For work over roughly 1500 Chinese characters, establish:

- One-sentence premise and reader promise
- Emotional curve: opening state, turn, peak, aftertaste
- Scene or chapter beats
- Character motivation and conflict
- Canon anchors and explicit AU deviations
- Ending hook or soft landing
- Content notes and likely tags

## Draft

- Anchor each scene in who wants what, what changes, and what the reader should feel.
- Use action, dialogue, and small sensory details before abstract explanation.
- Keep names, pronouns, honorifics, timeline, knowledge state, and relationship status stable.
- Let canon appear through setting and choices instead of decorative keyword repetition.
- Base physical details on canon or user-provided descriptions, not generic relationship-role templates.
- Let characters misunderstand, avoid, joke, deflect, or stay silent in character-specific ways.
- Save long work in controlled sections instead of producing an unreviewed wall of text.

## Continue

Before continuing, read project memory and reconstruct:

- Completed plot and current location/time
- Current emotional temperature and relationship state
- What each character knows, believes, and hides
- Unresolved conflict, promises, injuries, objects, and clues
- Required next beat and intended ending direction

After drafting, update `continuity.md` with only newly established facts and open loops.

## Revise

Preserve the user's voice and intent. Prioritize the three changes with the largest reader impact, then revise. Do not smooth every sentence into the same cadence or replace specific quirks with generic polished prose.

## Publish Pack

Prepare:

- Three to five title candidates, then one selected title
- One concise hook and one short summary
- Content notes or warnings when relevant
- Collection name and chapter number for serial work
- Accurate tags grouped by CP/character, IP/world, form, mood, and content
- A discussion prompt that invites a real response without begging for engagement
- AI disclosure and platform declaration reminder when AI materially assisted the work
- Rights/source and gift/monetization notes when relevant
