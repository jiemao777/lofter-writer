# Writing Mode Playbooks

Choose a mode from user intent. Combine modes only when the premise naturally needs both, and let one mode lead.

## Forum Thread

Use for public misunderstanding, gossip, identity reveal, group chaos, or fast comedy.

- Open with a situation and immediate question, not a plot synopsis.
- Escalate replies through jokes, clues, wrong guesses, and one or two sharp observers.
- Let new evidence change the crowd's interpretation.
- End on a reveal, reversal, consequence, or emotional landing.
- Give recurring posters distinct vocabulary, confidence, and blind spots.

Avoid exposition-only replies, identical commenter voices, and slang that erases character emotion.

## Chat Log

Use for intimacy, teasing, planning, group dynamics, or found-phone snippets.

- Establish participants and enough context to orient the reader.
- Use short bursts, interruption, silence, typo, callback, and delayed replies.
- Include image or file placeholders only when they change the scene.
- Make every speaker recognizable without relying only on names.

Avoid essay-length messages, unclear speakers, and identical sentence rhythms.

## Viewing Or Reaction

Use when characters watch canon, future, or alternate scenes and the appeal is changed understanding.

- State the viewing rule and boundary.
- Begin with a safe or funny clip, then destabilize assumptions.
- Let private reactions leak into public behavior.
- Make each viewed segment change a choice, relationship, or mission.

Avoid long canon retelling, repeated gasps, and spoilers that remove all remaining tension.

## Identity Reveal

Use for hidden relationships, secret competence, secret accounts, mistaken identity, or 掉马.

- Establish the mask and why exposure costs something.
- Seed clues that are legible in hindsight.
- Apply social or situational pressure.
- Reveal through action or evidence.
- Spend enough time on character-specific consequences.

Avoid cost-free reveals and instant universal acceptance.

## Daily Sweet Story

Use for comfort, domestic detail, healing, light romance, and low-conflict satisfaction.

- Start from a small ordinary problem.
- Show care through character-specific action or teasing.
- Add one sensory domestic anchor and one moment of hesitation.
- Land quietly instead of explaining the emotion.

Avoid generic pet names, interchangeable sweetness, and abstract-feeling endings after every paragraph.

## Comedy Bit

Use for short posts, meme-like premises, and easy sharing.

- Establish one absurd but clear premise.
- Escalate it in three materially different beats.
- Recontextualize the setup with one line or action.
- Add a soft final note only when it improves the aftertaste.

Avoid explaining the joke and adding side characters without a comic function.

## Canon Drama

Use for canon-adjacent plot, cases, missions, school, workplace, battlefield, or historical settings.

- Respect a canon-consistent setting constraint.
- Give the external problem real stakes.
- Reveal emotion through decisions under pressure.
- Carry consequences into the next scene or chapter.

Avoid incompatible modern slang and invented world facts presented with false certainty.

## Serial Collection

Use for multi-post projects.

- Define a stable series promise, motif, CP/focus, and collection identity.
- Let each chapter change plot state and relationship state.
- Vary cliffhanger types: decision, discovery, reversal, emotional fracture, or quiet implication.
- Update continuity files after every chapter.

Avoid silent changes to CP, tone, timeline, or core conflict.
