# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric after deterministic lint. The script finds mechanical risks; use judgment for literary quality.

## Review Order

1. List the three issues with the largest reader impact.
2. Explain the revision strategy briefly.
3. Revise the full text or a focused sample, depending on scope.
4. Recheck continuity and publication packaging.

Score from 1 to 5 only when the user asks for scoring or comparison.

## Story And Character

- Premise clarity: the reader quickly understands the situation and promise.
- Character consistency: dialogue, decisions, boundaries, and reactions fit canon and the brief.
- Relationship logic: closeness, conflict, teasing, and repair develop through observable choices.
- Knowledge state: characters do not react to facts they have not learned.
- Canon/world fit: details stay accurate or are marked as deliberate AU changes.
- Continuity: time, location, injuries, objects, promises, names, and pronouns remain stable.

## Scene And Voice

- Scene grounding: place, time, body position, props, and sensory details prevent floating dialogue.
- Dialogue texture: speakers differ in sentence length, humor, avoidance, vocabulary, and confidence.
- Pacing: setup, turn, peak, and aftertaste receive proportionate space.
- Subtext: action, silence, misdirection, and rhythm carry emotion before explanation does.
- Form execution: forum, chat, reaction, or mixed formats feel native rather than chopped-up prose.

## Editorial Specificity

- Replace repeated sentence frames and generalized emotion labels.
- Remove stock appearance descriptions and forced canon metaphors.
- Keep useful imperfection, hesitation, humor, and abruptness.
- Add concrete anchors where the scene floats, not decorative detail everywhere.
- Cut explanations that repeat what action or dialogue already establishes.
- Do not polish all characters into the same smooth voice.

This work improves specificity and authorship intent. Do not present it as a method to evade AI detection.

## Publication Fit

- Title and summary match the actual emotional promise.
- Tags are accurate, compact, and respectful of community etiquette.
- Warnings communicate material sensitive themes without sensationalism.
- AI assistance is disclosed when required.
- Copyright, real-person, minor, monetization, and platform risks receive the compliance pass.
