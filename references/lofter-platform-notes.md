## Publishing Constraints

- Treat tags as discovery and community signals, not traffic loopholes.
- Do not use unrelated hot tags, spam, fake engagement, misleading titles, or moderation-evasion techniques.
- Keep title, summary, warnings, tags, and the actual work aligned.
- Treat content and tags as subject to platform safety review.
- Do not promise recommendation, exposure, approval, or revenue.

## Tag Strategy

Group candidates by:

1. Main CP or relationship direction, when appropriate
2. Character tags, only when fandom etiquette permits
3. IP or world
4. Form, such as forum, chat, viewing, daily, short story, or serial
5. Mood, setting, and material content notes

Ask about known fandom customs when guessing could create CP-direction or character-tag conflict. Prefer a compact accurate set; the official algorithm explanation describes creator-added tags as generally ranging from 1 to 10.

## Titles And Summaries

- Focus titles on a premise, image, tension, or emotional hook.
- Avoid full-plot summaries, long dialogue chunks, and misleading relationship framing.
- Tell readers what kind of experience they are opening without building a synopsis wall.
- Add content notes for major canon divergence, spoilers, violence, death, self-harm themes, abuse, intense angst, or other material sensitivities.

## Collections And Interaction

Use stable, searchable collection names for a series, same-CP cluster, shared universe, or recurring format. Keep chapter numbering and core summary consistent.

Invite a specific response tied to the work, such as a clue, character choice, or next POV. Avoid empty requests for likes, follows, or comments.

Treat creator anecdotes about timing as experiments, not facts. Record comparable snapshots before claiming a posting window performs better.

## AI-Assisted Work

The current LOFTER community norms require AI-generated or AI-assisted text, images, and video to be clearly marked with `#AI生成#` or `#AI辅助#` and the platform's creation declaration. Therefore:

- Add a visible disclosure when AI materially assists the publishable work.
- Use platform-provided declaration controls honestly.
- Do not hide labels or metadata, disguise AI work as wholly natural creation, wash another creator's work, or flood tags with low-quality generated posts.
- Do not collect community works for model training without the required authorization.

## Gifts And Monetization

Gift features and eligibility can change. Verify current in-app terms, avoid guaranteed-income claims, and keep return gifts compliant and related to creator support. Paid fanwork can create additional copyright risk; recommend original, transformative, or authorized material and never use gifts for crowdfunding, gambling-like activity, or unrelated sales.
