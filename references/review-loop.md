# Performance Review Loop

Use this reference for `~review` and works-log analysis.

## Compare Fairly

- Compare snapshots taken at similar post ages, such as 24 hours with 24 hours or 7 days with 7 days.
- Do not compare a new post's raw totals with an older post's lifetime totals.
- Separate observations from explanations; performance data rarely proves why a post worked.
- Require at least three reasonably comparable works before treating a pattern as a useful hypothesis.
- Treat missing fields as missing. Never invent views, likes, recommendations, comments, or follower context.

## Useful Measures

When the platform exposes the inputs, calculate a directional interaction rate:

`(likes + recommendations + comments) / max(views, 1)`

Do not call this conversion, unique-reader engagement, or causal impact. Platform counters may not represent unique users.

Compare:

- Format and target emotional payoff
- Title type and opening type
- Tag set and collection membership
- Post age at snapshot
- Views and interaction rate
- Qualitative comment depth and recurring reader questions

## Recommend One Experiment

Change one main variable at a time, such as title type, opening speed, format, chapter length, tag set, or posting window. Keep CP/focus and quality bar stable where possible. Record the hypothesis before posting and the result at the same snapshot age.

Output:

1. What the comparable data shows
2. What remains uncertain
3. One hypothesis
4. One next experiment and its success measure
